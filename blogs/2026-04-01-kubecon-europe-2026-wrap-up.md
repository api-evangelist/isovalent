KubeCon Europe 2026 in Amsterdam felt less like a conference focused on Kubernetes and more like a glimpse into where the cloud native ecosystem is heading next. The conversations have shifted, and Kubernetes itself is no longer the main attraction. Instead, the focus has moved to the workloads running on top of it.
